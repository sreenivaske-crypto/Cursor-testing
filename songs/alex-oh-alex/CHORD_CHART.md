## How to play it in the room

- One acoustic guitar is enough. Strum: **down, down-up, up-down-up** (the folk "D-DU-UDU").
- Clap on **2 and 4** from the first chorus onward.
- Verses: one or two voices. Chorus: everybody.
- If you have no guitar, play the MP3 demo and sing over it — the flute line is the vocal melody.
