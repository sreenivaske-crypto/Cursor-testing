# Alex, Oh Alex (We're Gonna Miss You)

A farewell anthem for Alex.

**Style:** mid-tempo pop-folk singalong  
**Key:** G major  
**Tempo:** 104 BPM  
**Time:** 4/4  
**Feel:** warm, proud, a little bittersweet — built for a whole team to shout the chorus

---

## Short party version (about 90 seconds)

Sing **Verse 1 → Chorus → Bridge → Final Chorus → Outro**.

---

## Full song

*(soft, almost spoken — then the room joins the last line)*

Alex… oh Alex…  
We miss you…

Agility, rising under a brighter sun
